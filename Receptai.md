Here’s the recipe for my favorite Baileys whipped ganache. It’s perfect for decorating cakes, tarts, and cupcakes, as well as filling éclairs or choux pastries.

### Ingredients

100 g heavy whipped cream (33–36%) (1)
100 g dark chocolate (54.5%)

### Instructions

## Ferrero Rocher Chocolate Cookies

Sometimes you crave something truly rich, indulgent, and super chocolatey. That’s exactly what these Ferrero Rocher cookies are. Chocolate dough wrapped around a crunchy candy, with melted Nutella surrounding it. The result is a gooey cookie with a liquid hazelnut-chocolate center.

### Ingredients
190 g unsalted butter
290 g sugar
2 large eggs
50 g Dutch-processed cocoa powder
270 g all-purpose flour
1 tsp cornstarch
1 tsp baking soda
a pinch of salt
300 g milk chocolate (chips)
100 g chopped hazelnuts
10 Ferrero Rocher chocolate candies
Nutella (for filling)

### Instructions

In a mixer bowl, beat room-temperature butter with the paddle attachment on medium speed for about 2 minutes.

Add the sugar and beat for another 2–3 minutes, until the mixture is light and fluffy.

Add eggs one at a time, mixing on low speed until combined.

Add all dry ingredients (cocoa, flour, starch, baking soda, salt) and mix until fully combined.

Add chocolate chips and chopped hazelnuts, mix on low speed to incorporate.

### Shaping the cookies

Divide the dough into balls, about 100 g each.
Make an indentation in each ball, forming something like a “nest.”

Dip a Ferrero Rocher candy into Nutella. Place it in the center of the “nest” and carefully wrap the dough around it so it is completely covered.

Place the shaped dough balls in the refrigerator for 3–4 hours.

### Baking

Preheat the oven to 180°C (~350°F).
Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

Place the dough balls at least 2 cm (~1 in) apart.
Bake for 20–25 minutes.

### Storage

The baked cookies can be stored for 5–7 days in an airtight container at room temperature.

Raw dough balls can be frozen for up to 3 months. Before baking, place them directly on a baking sheet and bake slightly longer without thawing.

## RECIPE: PUMPKIN CREAM SOUP

Pumpkin cream soup with lentils is all about warmth, comfort, balanced flavor — and nourishment.

Its velvety texture, the gentle sweetness of butternut squash, hearty lentils, and aromatic spices make this soup perfect for cold days. Turkey adds protein and depth of flavor, while cream gives the soup a rich, silky consistency.

For 2.0–3.0 L of water:

Turkey thigh (700–800 g)
1 butternut squash (1.5–2.0 kg)
300–400 g red lentils
1 medium carrot
100–200 g pork fat for cracklings (optional) or 2 tbsp oil
1 medium onion
Salt to taste
Spices to taste (black pepper, smoked paprika, turmeric or curry, nutmeg)
200–250 g cream or cream cheese

### Method

1. Bring 2–3 L of water to a boil. Add the turkey thigh, chopped pumpkin and carrot, and 300–400 g of rinsed lentils. Simmer over low heat for 30–40 minutes.

2. In a frying pan, lightly fry the diced pork fat. Add the finely chopped onion and cook, stirring, until golden.
(If not using pork fat, sauté the onion in 2 tbsp of oil.)

3. Season with salt, pepper, paprika, turmeric, and nutmeg to taste. Pour in the cream or add the cream cheese, bring to a gentle boil, then remove from heat.

4. Remove the turkey thigh and cut the meat into small pieces. Blend the soup until smooth. Return the turkey pieces to the soup.

5. Serve topped with onion cracklings and pumpkin seeds.
Optionally, add croutons or shredded braised meat (veal).

## RECIPE: PUMPKIN MARBLE LOAF CAKE

⅔ tsp baking soda
½ tsp salt

½ tsp ground ginger
¼ tsp nutmeg

Chocolate batter:

15 g cocoa powder

### METHOD

## HAZELNUT PRALINE RECIPE

I’m sharing my trusted recipe for vanilla praline with you — and if you’re saving it, be sure to thank me with a  and leave a comment!

It’s quick to make, has a long shelf life, and tastes amazing in frostings, fillings, ganaches — or even just eaten by the spoonful!

- 250 g hazelnuts 2 used vanilla pods 120 g sugar 45 g glucose syrup 35 g water 1 tsp Fleur de Sel or ½ tsp regular salt

Place hazelnuts and vanilla pods in the oven at 140°C (~285°F) for 15 minutes.
Meanwhile, combine sugar, glucose syrup, and water in a saucepan and heat the syrup to 118°C (~244°F).
Once the syrup reaches this temperature, add vanilla pods and hot nuts, and begin caramelizing.
At a certain point, your syrup will completely crystallize. Don’t worry — that’s normal! Keep cooking until the sugar melts again and turns into a dry caramel.
Pour the caramelized hazelnuts onto a tray (ideally lined with a silicone mat or parchment paper), sprinkle with fleur de sel, and let cool.
Break into pieces and transfer to a blender or food processor. Blend until it becomes a smooth paste.

Yield: ~380 g of praline.

By the way, you can substitute almonds or peanuts for hazelnuts.

▪ Soak the gelatin in cold water and allow it to swell.

▪ In a saucepan, bring the milk to a boil, then add the glucose syrup and the squeezed gelatin.

▪ Carefully pour 1/3 of the boiling mixture into the melted chocolate in a thin stream, stirring intensively with a spatula until a homogeneous and glossy mass forms.

▪ Repeat the previous step two more times, incorporating the second portion of the boiling mixture into the chocolate, and then the remaining third portion.

▪ Pour in the cold heavy cream and process the mixture with a blender until it’s smooth.

▪ Cover the namelaka with cling film, ensuring it touches the surface of the namelaka, and place it in the fridge to stabilize for at least 12 hours.

Now, in advance of any questions you may have:

- Use chocolate and gelatin according to the percentage of cocoa. Instead of dark chocolate, for example, you can use 350 g of milk chocolate 40% + 5 g of gelatin or 340 g of white chocolate 34% + 4 g of gelatin.

- Leaf gelatin can be replaced with powdered gelatin by converting between different strengths of gelatin.

- Glucose syrup is added to give the namelaka a silky texture and extend its shelf life. If you prefer, you can replace it with honey or omit it altogether.

- If you don’t have an emulsion blender, use a regular hand blender, making an effort to avoid incorporating any air bubbles, or mix the mixture until it’s smooth using a silicone spatula.

Today I am sharing with you a recipe for an incredibly delicious coconut ganache which can be used as a filling for your cake. It seems that the coconut smell is all around me today, so here you go

To make coconut ganache you will need:

- 120 g fresh brown coconut pulp
- 50 g heavy cream 33–36%
- 150 g white chocolate 28–30%

- blender/food processor
- thick-bottomed saucepan, silicone spatula

### Method

- grind the peeled fresh coconut pulp into small pieces in a food processor
- place the ground pulp in a saucepan, pour in the cream and place the saucepan on low heat
- warm the mixture up until hot, but do not boil it
- pour the hot mixture over the chocolate, leave for 30–40 seconds, and mix thoroughly with a silicone spatula
- pour the finished ganache into a bowl, cover with cling film touching the surface of the ganache and let it cool slightly
- place in the fridge to stabilize for at least 5–6 hours, preferably overnight

- Mouthwatering ganache with a pleasant crunch of coconut pulp is ready!

You can use it as a filling for a sponge, Napoleon or honey cake, as I did when creating a Chiffon honey cake, which you can see in the carousel

## HOMEMADE HAZELNUT PASTE

#marusya_theory_tips

Today I will share with you some tips on how to easily and effortlessly make the hazelnut paste at home.

The first and most important tip for getting an incredibly tasty and fragrant hazelnut paste is to use WELL-TOASTED nuts!

- Got it?
- Brilliant!

To make the hazelnut paste you will need:

- 200 g hazelnuts
- blender/food processor

### Method

- Arrange hazelnuts on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Toast them in the oven preheated to 150 °C for around 20-30 minutes until golden brown, stirring occasionally after the nuts begin to release oil — you should feel a pleasant nutty aroma. Remove skins if necessary.

- I recommend using well-toasted nuts only for making nut paste — for other purposes, such high toasting level is not appropriate, since hazelnuts become slightly bitter.

- Place the well-toasted nuts in the bowl of a blender or food processor and process until they begin to actively release oil and acquire a pasty consistency.

- to get a uniform texture of the paste, use at least 200 g of toasted nuts (depending on the diameter of your blender bowl)
- in the process of grinding, don’t forget to stop the blender and mix the paste with a spatula occasionally, scraping it from the walls of the bowl
- to speed up the blending process, you can add a little odorless vegetable oil, but I usually don’t do this — firstly, the hazelnuts start to release oil during grinding, and secondly, extra oil makes the nut paste runny and, as a result, affects the texture of frosting or ganache made with the addition of nut paste

That's it — the hazelnut paste is ready!

Use the finished nut paste for making various types of fillings and ganaches — it will give them an unforgettable rich nutty flavor!

## HOW TO MAKE VANILLA POWDER AT HOME

#marusya_theory_tips

Today I will share with you a little secret on how to easily and effortlessly make natural vanilla powder at home.

It is made from scraped vanilla pods — usually we remove the seeds and use them to make a variety of fillings and frosting. But experienced confectioners know that empty pods can also be reused. For example, to make vanilla powder.

So, to make the vanilla powder you will need:

◽3 g scraped vanilla pods
◽30 g caster sugar

- coffee grinder

### Preparation

▫ Cut the vanilla pods in half lengthwise, remove the seeds (use them to make any dessert of your choice)
▫ dry the pods in the oven until brittle
- if the scraped pods have been stored for a long time and become dry, you can skip this step
▫cut the pods with scissors into medium pieces to fit in your coffee grinder
▫ add sugar
▫ grind everything into powder

That's it — the vanilla powder is ready!

You can use it to add an incredibly spiced aroma to your pastries!
