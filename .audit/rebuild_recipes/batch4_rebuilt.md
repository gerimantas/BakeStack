## RECIPE FOR PANETTONE WITH CANDIED TANGERINE 🍊

To celebrate the upcoming Easter, we'd like to share with you a recipe for panettone with incredibly juicy, soft and tender crumb!

So, grab your apron and enjoy working with an incredible panettone dough with a pleasant citrus aroma of candied tangerines!

#marusya_recipe

#### 🍊 Candied tangerines

150–200 g tangerine peel
2 g salt
250–300 g caster sugar
1 vanilla pod
80 g rum

#### 🥃 Rum soaked dried fruits

60 g golden raisins
60 g dried cranberries
60 g candied tangerines
150 g rum

#### 🥛 Bread starter

250 g milk 3.2%
7 g caster sugar
7–8 g dry yeast
150 g strong flour

#### 🍞 Panettone dough

165 g whole eggs (~3 pcs)
150 g caster sugar
1 vanilla pod
lemon zest
zest of 2 oranges
415 g bread starter
400 g strong flour
2 g salt
100 g butter 82%
330 g rum soaked dried fruits

### Instructions

#### ✔️ Candied tangerines

Wash and pat dry the tangerines. Remove the peel, cut it into small pieces, place in a saucepan and pour water on top so that it completely covers the peel.
📌 Add 2 g of salt, bring to a boil and simmer for about 2–5 minutes. Drain the water. Repeat the same steps 2–3 more times to get rid of the bitterness of the albedo.

📌 Place sugar in a thick-bottomed saucepan and add water so that it covers the sugar by ~ 0.5–1.0 cm. Add vanilla seeds, rum and warm everything up until the sugar dissolves completely. Add the tangerine peels. Bring to a boil and simmer until almost all of the liquid has evaporated.
📌 Arrange the candied tangerine on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and dry for ~ 1–2 hours in the oven (with the oven door slightly ajar) on minimum heat.

Wash and pat dry the dried fruits, mix with candied tangerine, pour in cognac / rum / limoncello or orange juice. Leave in the fridge overnight.

#### ✔️ Bread starter

📌 Pour the warm milk into a small bowl, add sugar, dry yeast, mix until smooth and leave for 5–7 minutes until the mixture foams a little. Add the sifted flour, carefully combine with a whisk and leave in a warm place for ~ 30–60 minutes until the dough doubles in volume.
📍 if desired, you can replace dry yeast with 25 g of fresh yeast

## RECIPE FOR THE PERFECT CHEESECAKE CRUMBLE

As you probably already know, I am a perfectionist by nature, and the story of creation of this recipe is closely related to this trait of mine.

Here is how it was. Once, when tasting my new cheesecake, my family, who used to be treated to nice desserts, ate the cream cheese layer with great pleasure, but left the crust almost untouched.

An irresistible desire to make tasters enjoy both the cream cheese layer and the shortbread, inspired me to create an incredibly tasty, pleasantly crunchy shortbread crumble that disappears from the plate in a matter of minutes:

### Ingredients

🟠 95 g butter 82% (1)
🟠 80 g of icing sugar
🟠 18 g egg yolks (~1 pc.)
🟠 150 g all-purpose flour
🟠 10 g cornstarch
🟠 70 g ground hazelnuts/almonds
🟠 2 g rock salt
🟠 10-20 g butter 82% (2)
🟠 20-30 g coconut oil
🟠 10 g milk 3.2%

### Instructions

📌 Mix soft butter (1) and icing sugar until combined. Add the yolk, mix. Add flour, sifted with cornstarch, ground nuts and salt, and mix in a mixer for 30–40 seconds until crumbs have formed. It is important not to mix the dough for a long time.
📌 Transfer the crumbs to a baking sheet lined with parchment paper, distribute them evenly and place in the freezer for at least 30 minutes. Bake in an oven, preheated to 170–180 °C, for about 15–18 minutes, then cool down completely. The well-baked shortbread crumbs should be crispy and golden brown.
📌 Place the crumble in a food processor or blender and process it in pulses. Add 10–20 g soft butter (2), 20–30 g coconut oil and 10 g milk, mix thoroughly.
📌 Grease the walls of the cake ring with coconut oil or regular butter (the bottom of the ring should be tightly covered with foil in advance). Form the base of the cheesecake by pressing the shortbread crumbs first against the sides and then — the bottom of the cake ring. Then level the crust and make it smooth using a bent tablespoon. Put in the freezer for at least 30 min.

#marusya_recipe

### Ingredients

🍮 50–150 g raisins (to taste)
🍮 1 kg cottage cheese 5–9%
🍮 10 g baking powder
🍮 2 g rock salt
🍮 300 g sour cream 15–25%
🍮 150 g semolina
🍮 440 g whole eggs (~8 pcs)
🍮 200 g of caster sugar
🍮 1 tsp. vanilla paste
🫐 fresh berries for decoration

### Instructions

🟡 Rinse the raisins thoroughly and place in a bowl. Pour the boiling water on top and leave for 15–20 minutes. Drain the water and pat dry the raisins.
🟣 Place cottage cheese in a large deep bowl, add baking powder, salt and mix.
🟡 In a separate container, combine sour cream with semolina and leave for 15–20 minutes to swell, then combine with the cottage cheese mass and mix until smooth.

🟡 Incorporate the whipped eggs into the cottage cheese mass in 2–3 lots, mixing thoroughly until smooth after each addition. Add the raisins and mix again.

## COCONUT GANACHE RECIPE 🥥

Would you like a recipe for ganache?

Today I am sharing with you a recipe for an incredibly delicious coconut ganache which can be used as a filling for your cake. It seems that the coconut smell is all around me today, so here you go☺️

To make coconut ganache you will need:

### Ingredients

🥥 120 g fresh brown coconut pulp
🥥 50 g heavy cream 33–36%
🥥 150 g white chocolate 28–30%

🔌 blender/food processor
🍴 thick-bottomed saucepan, silicone spatula

### Instructions

Method:

⚪️ grind the peeled fresh coconut pulp into small pieces in a food processor
🟤 place the ground pulp in a saucepan, pour in the cream and place the saucepan on low heat
⚪️ warm the mixture up until hot, but do not boil it
🟤 pour the hot mixture over the chocolate, leave for 30–40 seconds, and mix thoroughly with a silicone spatula
⚪️ pour the finished ganache into a bowl, cover with cling film touching the surface of the ganache and let it cool slightly
🟤 place in the fridge to stabilize for at least 5–6 hours, preferably overnight

## HOMEMADE HAZELNUT PASTE

**NOTE:** This entry is a "theory/tips" post (tagged `#marusya_theory_tips`), not a standard recipe with a narrative intro — but it does have a clear ingredient list and numbered method, structured the same as a recipe, so I've included it as its own entry per the instruction to identify "distinct recipes" by content/subject change.

#marusya_theory_tips

Today I will share with you some tips on how to easily and effortlessly make the hazelnut paste at home.

The first and most important tip for getting an incredibly tasty and fragrant hazelnut paste is to use WELL-TOASTED nuts!

❓Got it?
✔️Brilliant!

To make the hazelnut paste you will need:

### Ingredients

🔺200 g hazelnuts
🔌 blender/food processor

### Instructions

Method:

🔺Arrange hazelnuts on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Toast them in the oven preheated to 150 °C for around 20-30 minutes until golden brown, stirring occasionally after the nuts begin to release oil — you should feel a pleasant nutty aroma. Remove skins if necessary.

🌰I recommend using well-toasted nuts only for making nut paste — for other purposes, such high toasting level is not appropriate, since hazelnuts become slightly bitter.

🔺 Place the well-toasted nuts in the bowl of a blender or food processor and process until they begin to actively release oil and acquire a pasty consistency.

🌰 to get a uniform texture of the paste, use at least 200 g of toasted nuts (depending on the diameter of your blender bowl)
🌰 in the process of grinding, don't forget to stop the blender and mix the paste with a spatula occasionally, scraping it from the walls of the bowl
🌰 to speed up the blending process, you can add a little odorless vegetable oil, but I usually don't do this — firstly, the hazelnuts start to release oil during grinding, and secondly, extra oil makes the nut paste runny and, as a result, affects the texture of frosting or ganache made with the addition of nut paste

That's it — the hazelnut paste is ready!

Use the finished nut paste for making various types of fillings and ganaches — it will give them an unforgettable rich nutty flavor!

Each of us is familiar with the taste of bright, juicy strawberries disturbing our imagination and appetite. But do we know everything about its aroma profile? What will it pair well with?

Let's do a foodpairing autopsy of the favorite of millions🔝

🔴 Flavor Description
▪️ sweet "candy"
▪️ nice acidity
▪️ top fruity notes
▪️ light creamy aftertaste
▪️ caramel notes
▪️ inherent oiliness

Strawberries can be leading — then you need weaker flavors that emphasize the intensity of its taste, sweetness. If strawberries are as addition, beat the leading flavor with spices or heat treatment.

🔴 Aroma Profile
▪️ fruity aroma
▪️ sweet caramel notes
▪️ light grassiness
▪️ spicy notes of vanilla
▪️ strong resemblance to tomato aromatics

The aroma profile of strawberries is composed of over 360 components, including esters, aldehydes, ketones, alcohols, terpenes, furanones, and sulfur compounds. The aroma of ripe strawberries is recognized as one of the most complex fruit flavors.

Pairs well with:

🔻 nuts — almonds, cashews, pistachios and pecans. The ideal nutty companion — hazelnuts — does not overlap the taste of strawberries, but gently complements it.

🔻 other fruits and berries such as orange, pineapple, melon, raspberry, coconut

The flavor combination 🍓🍅 strawberry-tomato deserves special attention — the natural flavor of strawberries is very similar to the aromatics of tomatoes, so the presence of tomatoes in dessert tarts, stracciatella and bruschettas is increasingly being traced

🔻 spices, herbs and seasonings — vanilla, cinnamon, mint, ginger, lemongrass and... balsamic vinegar! Just a couple of drops of balsamic can greatly enhance the taste of strawberries. The combination is often used by chefs in salads, tarts.

🔻 dairy products – cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, cream, mascarpone, cream cheese
🍓Strawberry-Soft Cheeses — strawberries have soft, creamy, buttery notes in their flavor, making them a great partner for soft milk, creamy, cheeses and cottage cheese.

## HOW TO MAKE VANILLA POWDER AT HOME

**NOTE:** Another "theory/tips" post (`#marusya_theory_tips`), not a narrative recipe, but structured with an ingredient-like list and numbered method — included as its own entry per the same logic as "HOMEMADE HAZELNUT PASTE" above.

#marusya_theory_tips

Today I will share with you a little secret on how to easily and effortlessly make natural vanilla powder at home.

It is made from scraped vanilla pods — usually we remove the seeds and use them to make a variety of fillings and frosting. But experienced confectioners know that empty pods can also be reused. For example, to make vanilla powder.

So, to make the vanilla powder you will need:

### Ingredients

◽️3 g scraped vanilla pods
◽️30 g caster sugar

🔌 coffee grinder

### Instructions

Preparation:

▫️ Cut the vanilla pods in half lengthwise, remove the seeds (use them to make any dessert of your choice)
