## 1. [UNTITLED: Baileys Whipped Ganache]

**NOTE:** No title line is present for this recipe at the very start of the source document — the text begins mid-context ("Here's the recipe for my favorite Baileys whipped ganache...") with the document's first two lines blank. Confirmed by direct inspection of the raw source: line 1 of the file is genuinely the first content, so there is no earlier title lost to batch-range clipping.

Here's the recipe for my favorite Baileys whipped ganache. It's perfect for decorating cakes, tarts, and cupcakes, as well as filling éclairs or choux pastries.

### Ingredients

100 g heavy whipped cream (33–36%) (1)
100 g dark chocolate (54.5%)
160 g heavy whipped cream (33–36%) (2)
20 g Baileys liqueur
50 g mascarpone (80–85%)

### Instructions

1. Pour 100 g of cream (1) into a saucepan and heat until just before boiling (do not let it boil). Pour the hot cream over the chocolate callets and let sit for 30–40 seconds. Blend thoroughly with an immersion blender until smooth and fully homogenous.

2. Add the cold cream (2) and Baileys liqueur. Blend again with the immersion blender. Cover the entire surface of the ganache with plastic food wrap.

3. Refrigerate for at least 10–12 hours (preferably overnight) for the ganache to set.
4. Transfer the chilled ganache to a mixing bowl, add the mascarpone, and whip with a hand mixer fitted with a whisk attachment until light, fluffy, and stable.

## 2. Ferrero Rocher Chocolate Cookies

Sometimes you crave something truly rich, indulgent, and super chocolatey. That's exactly what these Ferrero Rocher cookies are. Chocolate dough wrapped around a crunchy candy, with melted Nutella surrounding it. The result is a gooey cookie with a liquid hazelnut-chocolate center.

### Ingredients

190 g unsalted butter
290 g sugar
2 large eggs
50 g Dutch-processed cocoa powder
270 g all-purpose flour
1 tsp cornstarch
1 tsp baking soda
a pinch of salt
300 g milk chocolate (chips)
100 g chopped hazelnuts
10 Ferrero Rocher chocolate candies
Nutella (for filling)

### Instructions

In a mixer bowl, beat room-temperature butter with the paddle attachment on medium speed for about 2 minutes.

Add the sugar and beat for another 2–3 minutes, until the mixture is light and fluffy.

Add eggs one at a time, mixing on low speed until combined.

Add all dry ingredients (cocoa, flour, starch, baking soda, salt) and mix until fully combined.

Add chocolate chips and chopped hazelnuts, mix on low speed to incorporate.

#### Shaping the cookies:

Divide the dough into balls, about 100 g each.
Make an indentation in each ball, forming something like a "nest."

Dip a Ferrero Rocher candy into Nutella. Place it in the center of the "nest" and carefully wrap the dough around it so it is completely covered.

Place the shaped dough balls in the refrigerator for 3–4 hours.

#### Baking:

Preheat the oven to 180°C (~350°F).
Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

Place the dough balls at least 2 cm (~1 in) apart.
Bake for 20–25 minutes.

#### Storage:

The baked cookies can be stored for 5–7 days in an airtight container at room temperature.

Raw dough balls can be frozen for up to 3 months. Before baking, place them directly on a baking sheet and bake slightly longer without thawing.

## 3. BLUEBERRY-LEMON ROLLS RECIPE

My recent obsession—the floral notes of blueberry paired with the explosive tang of lemon. The combination of the aroma of a fresh buttery bun with the scent of lemon zest and the delicate fragrance of blueberries is going to make your head spin!

### Ingredients

#### ✔️Dough

180 g warm milk
7 g dry yeast
50 g sugar
60 g melted unsalted butter
1 egg + 1 yolk
zest of 2 🍋
4 g salt
360 g strong flour (I use Zernari Select, bread flour will work)

#### ✔️Optional Bottom Layer Icing (for extra tenderness and juiciness)

20 g soft unsalted butter
🍋 zest

#### ✔️Blueberry filling

300 g blueberries
60 g sugar
40 g 🍋 pieces
16 g cornstarch
40 g 🍋 juice

#### ✔️Lemon curd

60 g sugar
60 g yolks
90 g lemon juice
🍋 zest
6–9 g cornstarch
50 g unsalted butter

#### ✔️Icing

60 g soft unsalted butter
100 g sugar
2 g salt
10 g 🍋 juice
🍋 zest

#### ✔️Frosting

250 g cream cheese or mascarpone
100 g heavy whipping cream (33–36%)
20 g blueberry filling

### Instructions

1. Mix warm milk, sugar, butter, and yeast. Add the egg, yolk, zest, and salt. Stir in the flour and knead into a soft, sticky dough — cover and let rise in a warm place (~1–2 hours).

2. Place the blueberries in a saucepan over medium heat. Add sugar and lemon pieces. Dissolve the starch in the juice and pour into the mixture. Stirring constantly, cook until it comes to a boil.

3. For the curd, place all ingredients in a saucepan, combine thoroughly, and while stirring bring to a boil. Strain through a sieve.

4. For the bottom icing layer, combine the butter with zest. Grease the bottom of the pan with it(~35–40 × 25–30 cm).

5. For the spread, combine the butter with sugar, salt, juice, and zest.

6. Roll out the dough into a thin rectangle and evenly spread the butter mixture over it. Add the blueberry filling (set aside 20 g).

7. Roll into a log, cut into 8 pieces, and place in a baking dish. Let rise for another ~1 hour. Bake at 175°C (~350°F) for 25–30 minutes.

8. Whip cream cheese with the heavy whipping cream and remaining blueberry filling. Spread over the still-warm buns, make an indentation in the center, and place 1–2 tbsp of lemon curd inside. Decorate with blueberries.

## 4. MANDARIN-PASSIONFRUIT CUPCAKES

These aren't just cupcakes — they're a firework of flavor! A celebration, unforgettable bliss, and an endorphin injection all in a single bite

55g butter (82%)
120g odorless oil
300g sugar
4 large eggs
1 tsp vanilla paste/extract
330g all-purpose flour

#### FROSTING

240g passionfruit purée

### Instructions

#### BATTER

1.Place softened butter, oil, and sugar in the mixer bowl. Beat for about 5 min., until the mixture becomes pale and fluffy
2.Add eggs one at a time, then mix in vanilla
3.Sift and combine dry ingredients
4.Reduce mixer speed and add half of the dry ingredients
5.Add kefir, mix thoroughly
6.Add the remaining dry ingredients and combine
7.Distribute batter into cupcake liners, filling them about 2/3 full. Bake in a preheated oven at 170–180°C (~340–355°F) for 20–25 min

2.Add cream, stir, cover with food wrap, ensure it touches the entire surface, and refrigerate overnight
3.Add mascarpone and whip until frosting reaches a stable consistency

#### Assembly

Scoop out the center of cupcakes, soak with 2–3 tsp of Cointreau, add filling, frost, and decorate. You can use rum instead of Cointreau

## 5. RECIPE: PUMPKIN CREAM SOUP 🍲

Pumpkin cream soup with lentils is all about warmth, comfort, balanced flavor — and nourishment.

Its velvety texture, the gentle sweetness of butternut squash, hearty lentils, and aromatic spices make this soup perfect for cold days. Turkey adds protein and depth of flavor, while cream gives the soup a rich, silky consistency.

### Ingredients

For 2.0–3.0 L of water:

Turkey thigh (700–800 g)
1 butternut squash (1.5–2.0 kg)
300–400 g red lentils
1 medium carrot
100–200 g pork fat for cracklings (optional) or 2 tbsp oil
1 medium onion
Salt to taste
Spices to taste (black pepper, smoked paprika, turmeric or curry, nutmeg)
200–250 g cream or cream cheese

### Instructions

#### Method:

1. Bring 2–3 L of water to a boil. Add the turkey thigh, chopped pumpkin and carrot, and 300–400 g of rinsed lentils. Simmer over low heat for 30–40 minutes.

2. In a frying pan, lightly fry the diced pork fat. Add the finely chopped onion and cook, stirring, until golden.
(If not using pork fat, sauté the onion in 2 tbsp of oil.)

3. Season with salt, pepper, paprika, turmeric, and nutmeg to taste. Pour in the cream or add the cream cheese, bring to a gentle boil, then remove from heat.

4. Remove the turkey thigh and cut the meat into small pieces. Blend the soup until smooth. Return the turkey pieces to the soup.

5. Serve topped with onion cracklings and pumpkin seeds.
Optionally, add croutons or shredded braised meat (veal).

## 6. RECIPE: PUMPKIN MARBLE LOAF CAKE 🎃

An ideal dessert for the colder season, combining the gentle sweetness of spiced pumpkin with the warming richness of chocolate.

A simple recipe that creates a cozy, comforting atmosphere ❄️

### Ingredients

#### INGREDIENTS

200 g sugar
115 g melted butter
225 g thick pumpkin purée
75 g milk (room temperature)
70 g eggs (room temperature)
175 g flour
1 tsp baking powder
⅔ tsp baking soda
½ tsp salt
1 tsp cinnamon
½ tsp ground ginger
¼ tsp nutmeg

#### Chocolate batter:

15 g cocoa powder
15 g milk (room temperature)

### Instructions

#### METHOD

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (356°F). Line a loaf pan (~23 × 10 cm) with parchment paper.
In a large bowl, combine the sugar, melted butter, pumpkin purée, milk, and eggs.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together all the dry ingredients: flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and spices.
3. Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and mix until smooth and fully combined.
In a separate bowl, mix ⅓ of the pumpkin batter with the cocoa powder and milk until smooth.
4. Spoon the batters into the pan, alternating layers of pumpkin and chocolate batter. Gently tap the pan to evenly distribute the batter.
5. Bake at 180°C for about 70–80 minutes, until risen and golden. Check for doneness with a skewer.
6. Let the cake cool in the pan for 10–15 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

P.S. To achieve a thick pumpkin purée, roast the pumpkin in small pieces at 180°C (356°F) with convection for about 45–60 minutes.

## 7. Tart Cherry-Coconut Swirls

Who else loves baking with tart cherries as much as I do?—Today is your day! These soft, fluffy, feather-light sweet swirls with crunchy coconut flakes and the juicy tartness of ripe cherries just melt in your mouth, delivering incredible pleasure!

### Ingredients

#### ✔️Dough

180 g warm milk
7 g dry yeast
50 g granulated sugar
60 g melted unsalted butter
1 egg + 1 yolk
4 g salt
360 g strong flour (I use Zernari Select, any bread flour will work)

#### ✔️Optional Bottom Layer Icing (for extra tenderness and juiciness)

50 g soft unsalted butter
50 g granulated sugar
2 g salt
25 g coconut flakes

#### ✔️Tart cherry filling

500 g pitted tart cherries
80 g granulated sugar
20 g cornstarch
25 g lemon juice

#### ✔️Spread

60 g soft unsalted butter
100 g granulated sugar
2 g salt
50 g coconut flakes

#### ✔️Icing

250 g cream cheese or mascarpone
150 g heavy whipping cream 33–36% or coconut cream 12–18%
30 g coconut flakes

### Instructions

#### 📌Instructions

1. Mix warm milk, sugar, butter, and yeast. Add egg, yolk, and salt. Gradually mix in the flour, then knead into a soft, sticky dough—cover and let rise in a warm place (~1–2 hours).

2. Place tart cherries in a saucepan over medium heat. Add sugar. Dissolve the cornstarch in the lemon juice, then stir it into the mixture. Cook, stirring, until it boils.

3. For the bottom layer icing (optional), mix soft butter with sugar, salt, and coconut flakes. Spread on the bottom of a pan (~35–40 × 25–30 cm).

4. For the spread, grind the coconut flakes in a coffee grinder into a paste. Mix butter with sugar, salt, and coconut paste.

5. Roll out the dough into a thin rectangular sheet and evenly spread the spread over it. Add 1/2 of the cherry filling.

6. Roll into a log, cut into 8 pieces, and place into a baking dish. Let it rise again for another hour. Bake at 175°C  (~350°F) for 25–30 minutes.

7. Whip cream cheese or mascarpone with heavy cream and coconut flakes. Spread the icing on warm swirls, forming a slight indentation in the center, and place 1 tbsp of cherry filling in it. Sprinkle with coconut flakes.

## 10. TIRAMISU CHEESECAKE WITH BAILEYS

Imagine a meeting of two legends — tiramisu and cheesecake — a crispy shortcrust base, silky creamy mascarpone and Baileys filling, aromatic coffee and liqueur generously soaking the Savoiardi biscuits, and a light bitter cocoa finish.

### Ingredients

#### 📌 Shortcrust dough (large portion for ~3–4 cheesecakes)

375 g all purpose flour
45 g almond flour
230 g unsalted butter
60 g eggs
127 g powdered sugar
2 g salt

#### 📌 Base

200–230 g shortcrust crumbs
15 g almond flakes
90 g melted unsalted butter
30 g chopped hazelnuts
1 tsp brown sugar (optional)

#### 📌 Cheese filling

500 g mascarpone
50 g sugar
75 g Baileys liqueur
300 g heavy whipping cream 33–36%
1 tsp vanilla extract

#### 📌 Savoiardi layer

~200–220 g Savoiardi cookies
75 g espresso
75 g Baileys liqueur

#### 📌 Decoration

200 g heavy whipping cream 30–33%
Cocoa

### Instructions

#### 👉 Shortcrust dough

1. Combine soft butter and powdered sugar using a paddle or whisk attachment. Add almond flour and mix.

2. Add half the regular flour and half the eggs, mix. Add the remaining eggs and flour, gently combine.

3. Wrap in plastic wrap in a thin layer and chill. Bake at 175°C until golden, breaking the dough into small pieces first.

#### 👉 Cheesecake

1. In a blender, add pieces of shortcrust dough and almond flakes, grind to fine crumbs.

2. Add chopped hazelnuts, sugar, and melted butter. Mix thoroughly.

3. Line the bottom of a ring mold with foil and the sides with acetate sheet, then press the base mixture tightly.

4. Place mascarpone, sugar, Baileys, and vanilla extract in a mixer bowl and gently mix until smooth.

5. Add cream and whisk to thick consistency (or whip separately and fold in gently).

6. Spread half the cheese mixture over the base. Soak Savoiardi in coffee and liqueur and place the biscuit layer.

7. Spread the remaining cheese mixture on top, smooth evenly, and refrigerate overnight to set.

8. Decorate with whipped cream and cocoa.

## 18. Banana Whipped Ganache 🍌

🤤 If you've been looking for a reliable and stable frosting that you also want to eat straight from the bowl with a spoon—here it is!

### Instructions

## 19. MATCHA STRAWBERRY TIRAMISU 🍓

This is the moment when the flavor goes beyond expectations — light, refreshing, and perfectly summery. 🤤

The gentle herbal bitterness of matcha and juicy, fragrant strawberries create an incredible flavor duet.

💬 Even those who don't usually like matcha say: "This is so delicious!"

Be sure to save the recipe and don't forget to thank me in the comments!🫶🏻

### Ingredients

#### FROSTING

6 egg yolks (~100 g)
130 g granulated sugar
500 g mascarpone
15 g matcha
400 g heavy whipping cream (33–35%)

350–400 g ladyfingers (savoiardi)

#### SOAKING SYRUP

15 g matcha
50 g granulated sugar
70 g hot water (~80°C/~176°F)
210 g milk

#### FILLING

400–450 g fresh strawberries, diced

~10 g matcha for dusting

### INSTRUCTIONS

1. Mix the yolks and sugar into a heatproof bowl, and set over a hot water bath.

2. Stir constantly with a whisk, heat to 60°C (~140°F), and hold over a water bath for another 2–3 minutes to pasteurize the yolks.

3. Transfer to a mixer bowl, let cool to room temperature, and beat until the yolks turn light and pale and increase in volume.

4. Add mascarpone and matcha; mix until smooth.

5. In a separate bowl, whip the cold heavy whipping cream to soft peaks, then gently fold into the yolk mixture with a spatula.

6. For the soak, combine matcha and sugar, pour over with hot water, and mix thoroughly with a milk frother or spiral whisk. Add the milk and mix again.

7. Spread a thin layer of frosting on the bottom of your dish. Quickly dip half (~175–200 g) of the Savoiardi biscuits in the soaking syrup and arrange as the first layer.

8. Evenly spread 1/4 of the frosting on top. Then add a layer of diced strawberries and cover with another 1/4 of the frosting.

9. Soak the remaining Savoiardi biscuits and lay out the second Tiramisu layer.

10. Evenly spread the remaining half of the frosting on top and generously dust with matcha through a sieve.

11. Chill in the fridge for at least 10–12 hours for the Tiramisu to set

## 25. HAZELNUT PRALINE RECIPE

I'm sharing my trusted recipe for vanilla praline with you — and if you're saving it, be sure to thank me with a ❤️ and leave a comment!

It's quick to make, has a long shelf life, and tastes amazing in frostings, fillings, ganaches — or even just eaten by the spoonful!

### Ingredients

✔️250 g hazelnuts ✔️2 used vanilla pods ✔️120 g sugar ✔️45 g glucose syrup ✔️35 g water ✔️1 tsp Fleur de Sel or ½ tsp regular salt

### Instructions

Place hazelnuts and vanilla pods in the oven at 140°C (~285°F) for 15 minutes.
Meanwhile, combine sugar, glucose syrup, and water in a saucepan and heat the syrup to 118°C (~244°F).
Once the syrup reaches this temperature, add vanilla pods and hot nuts, and begin caramelizing.
At a certain point, your syrup will completely crystallize. Don't worry — that's normal! Keep cooking until the sugar melts again and turns into a dry caramel.
Pour the caramelized hazelnuts onto a tray (ideally lined with a silicone mat or parchment paper), sprinkle with fleur de sel, and let cool.
Break into pieces and transfer to a blender or food processor. Blend until it becomes a smooth paste.

Yield: ~380 g of praline.

By the way, you can substitute almonds or peanuts for hazelnuts.

Now, in advance of any questions you may have:

## 45. [TECHNIQUE, NOT A DISH] How to Make Perfect Chocolate Drips

Chocolate drips are an elegant and seemingly simple decoration for a cake, in fact, they also require certain knowledge and skills.

### Ingredients

#### Step 1. Dark Chocolate Drip Icing
You will need:

⚫️ 75 g dark chocolate 54.5%
⚫️ 25 g of odorless vegetable oil
🍴 heat-resistant silicone spatula
🍴 plastic bowl

#### Step 2. Applying the Drips
You will need:

🍴 pastry bag
🍴 scissors
🍴 offset spatula

### Instructions

#### Step 1. Dark Chocolate Drip Icing
While stirring, melt the chocolate over a water bath or in pulses in the microwave oven, add the vegetable oil and mix thoroughly until completely smooth.
▪️ melt the chocolate in short pulses, 5–8 seconds, stirring vigorously with a spatula after each pulse
▪️ do not overheat the chocolate, otherwise it will curdle – for dark chocolate, the maximum temperature is 50–55 °C
▪️before applying to the cake, the temperature of the icing should be 32–34 °C – if it is slightly higher or lower, you'll get either thick and rough drips (the icing isn't warm enough) or very thin translucent drips (overheated icing)

#### Step 2. Applying the Drips
Pour the chocolate icing into a piping bag and make a small cut. Pipe the slightly warm icing on the edge of the chilled cake, allowing some of the icing to roll off the edge and run down the side. Pour the rest of the icing into the center of the cake and spread it over with an offset spatula.
▪️to get neat drips, it's more convenient to use a pastry bag rather than a spoon – you'll be able to adjust the thickness of the drips by making a cut bigger
▪️ the cake should be well chilled as the cold surface slows down the drips, preventing them from running down the entire length of the cake
▪️apply drips at the same distance from each other, but make them of different length, adjusting it with the amount of icing piped out

## 57. [TECHNIQUE, NOT A DISH] Homemade Hazelnut Paste

**NOTE:** This entry is a "theory/tips" post (tagged `#marusya_theory_tips`), not a standard narrative recipe — but it has a clear ingredient list and numbered method, structured the same as a recipe, so it is included as its own entry, marked as a technique per the task instructions.

#marusya_theory_tips

Today I will share with you some tips on how to easily and effortlessly make the hazelnut paste at home.

The first and most important tip for getting an incredibly tasty and fragrant hazelnut paste is to use WELL-TOASTED nuts!

❓Got it?
✔️Brilliant!

To make the hazelnut paste you will need:

### Ingredients

🔺200 g hazelnuts
🔌 blender/food processor

### Instructions

Method:

🔺Arrange hazelnuts on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Toast them in the oven preheated to 150 °C for around 20-30 minutes until golden brown, stirring occasionally after the nuts begin to release oil — you should feel a pleasant nutty aroma. Remove skins if necessary.

🌰I recommend using well-toasted nuts only for making nut paste — for other purposes, such high toasting level is not appropriate, since hazelnuts become slightly bitter.

🔺 Place the well-toasted nuts in the bowl of a blender or food processor and process until they begin to actively release oil and acquire a pasty consistency.

🌰 to get a uniform texture of the paste, use at least 200 g of toasted nuts (depending on the diameter of your blender bowl)
🌰 in the process of grinding, don't forget to stop the blender and mix the paste with a spatula occasionally, scraping it from the walls of the bowl
🌰 to speed up the blending process, you can add a little odorless vegetable oil, but I usually don't do this — firstly, the hazelnuts start to release oil during grinding, and secondly, extra oil makes the nut paste runny and, as a result, affects the texture of frosting or ganache made with the addition of nut paste

That's it — the hazelnut paste is ready!

Use the finished nut paste for making various types of fillings and ganaches — it will give them an unforgettable rich nutty flavor!
