## BANANA TEA CAKE WITH DATES AND NUTS

This cake is a true harmony of texture and flavor. The sweetness of bananas and dates creates a juicy base, while dark rum adds depth and subtle caramel notes. Crunchy walnuts bring balance and a lovely bite. Simple to make, yet looks and tastes like a showstopper 💯

### Ingredients (for two tea cake):

260 g chopped dates
200 g brown sugar
80 g butter 82%
60 g dark rum
180 g water
1/2 tsp. baking soda
80 g whole eggs
130 g bananas
1 tsp. vanilla paste
100 g walnuts
1 tsp. salt
260 g all-purpose flour
10 g baking powder

### Instructions

📌Lightly roast the walnuts in the oven at 170-180°C for about 5-6 minutes until light golden color and pleasant aroma. Let them cool down and coarse chop them.
📌Put sugar, butter, water, rum and chopped dates
in a saucepan. Stirring occasionally, heat the mixture until sugar and butter have dissolved. Then increase the heat and bring the mixture to a boil.
📌Remove the saucepan from the heat, pour the mixture into a large bowl and let it cool down to room temperature.
👉🏻if you do not want to use alcohol, you can replace it with water
📌In a separate bowl, beat eggs, banana puree and
vanilla paste until fluffy. Pour into the cooled mixture, add baking soda and mix until smooth. Add chopped walnuts, and mix again.
📌Combine flour, baking powder and salt, sift. Add
to the batter and mix until smooth.
📌Grease the molds with butter and dust them with
some flour. Pour the batter into the molds, filling them approximately ⅔ (~ 680 g of batter per 1 tea cake).
📌Bake at 180°C for 40-50 minutes. Check if the tea cakes are ready with a wooden skewer. Take the tea cakes out of the oven, let them cool down for about 5 mins and carefully remove them from of the molds. Place on a wire rack to cool down.

## Banana Whipped Ganache 🍌

🤤 If you've been looking for a reliable and stable frosting that you also want to eat straight from the bowl with a spoon—here it is!

Delicate, not overly sweet, super light, and incredibly delicious. And it comes with a rich banana flavor ⬇️

### Ingredients

#### Banana-infused cream:
• 230 g heavy whipping cream (33–36%)
• ~150–200 g very ripe bananas

#### Banana whipped ganache:
• 1–2 g gelatin
• 95 g banana cream (1)
• 100 g white chocolate
• 105 g banana cream (2)
• 325 g mascarpone
• 5 g vanilla paste

### Instructions

#### Banana-infused cream:
1)Cut the very ripe bananas into pieces, pour the cream over them, cover with plastic wrap, and refrigerate overnight to do the cold infusion.
2)Strain the banana-infused cream through a sieve. Don't throw away the bananas—you can use them to bake a delicious banana bread.

#### Banana whipped ganache:
1)Soak the gelatin in cold water and let it bloom.
2)In a saucepan, bring 95 g of the banana cream (1) to until just boiling (do not boil). Pour it over the white chocolate callets, let sit for 30–40 seconds, then stir with a silicone spatula until smooth.

❗️You can adjust the amount of gelatin to change the firmness of the frosting to your liking. Also, it's a good idea to adapt the gelatin amount depending on the cocoa butter content in your chocolate.
3)Melt the bloomed gelatin in the microwave, then pour it into the cream–chocolate mixture and mix thoroughly.
4)Add the remaining 105 g of banana cream (2), stir, and cover the entire surface of the ganache with plastic food wrap. Refrigerate for at least 10–12 hours to set.
5)Transfer the chilled ganache to a mixer bowl, add the mascarpone and vanilla paste (or seeds from a vanilla bean), and whip until it turns into a stable creamy mass.

## MATCHA STRAWBERRY TIRAMISU 🍓

This is the moment when the flavor goes beyond expectations — light, refreshing, and perfectly summery. 🤤

The gentle herbal bitterness of matcha and juicy, fragrant strawberries create an incredible flavor duet.

💬 Even those who don't usually like matcha say: "This is so delicious!"

Be sure to save the recipe and don't forget to thank me in the comments!🫶🏻

### Ingredients

#### FROSTING

6 egg yolks (~100 g)
130 g granulated sugar
500 g mascarpone
15 g matcha
400 g heavy whipping cream (33–35%)

350–400 g ladyfingers (savoiardi)

#### SOAKING SYRUP

15 g matcha
50 g granulated sugar
70 g hot water (~80°C/~176°F)
210 g milk

#### FILLING

400–450 g fresh strawberries, diced

~10 g matcha for dusting

### INSTRUCTIONS

1. Mix the yolks and sugar into a heatproof bowl, and set over a hot water bath.

2. Stir constantly with a whisk, heat to 60°C (~140°F), and hold over a water bath for another 2–3 minutes to pasteurize the yolks.

3. Transfer to a mixer bowl, let cool to room temperature, and beat until the yolks turn light and pale and increase in volume.

4. Add mascarpone and matcha; mix until smooth.

5. In a separate bowl, whip the cold heavy whipping cream to soft peaks, then gently fold into the yolk mixture with a spatula.

6. For the soak, combine matcha and sugar, pour over with hot water, and mix thoroughly with a milk frother or spiral whisk. Add the milk and mix again.

7. Spread a thin layer of frosting on the bottom of your dish. Quickly dip half (~175–200 g) of the Savoiardi biscuits in the soaking syrup and arrange as the first layer.

8. Evenly spread 1/4 of the frosting on top. Then add a layer of diced strawberries and cover with another 1/4 of the frosting.

9. Soak the remaining Savoiardi biscuits and lay out the second Tiramisu layer.

10. Evenly spread the remaining half of the frosting on top and generously dust with matcha through a sieve.

11. Chill in the fridge for at least 10–12 hours for the Tiramisu to set

## BAILEYS TIRAMISU

Classic tiramisu is already truly special: soft, creamy, rich with coffee flavor. But add a touch of Baileys—and it turns into pure indulgence that's dangerously addictive ✨

Here's the recipe for a perfectly balanced, decadent tiramisu that will definitely earn you tons of compliments!
Save it, make it—and let me know what you think of this variation! 🫶🏻

### Ingredients

#### FROSTING

•6 egg yolks (~100 g)
•100–120 g sugar
•500 g mascarpone
•Seeds of 1 vanilla bean
•450 g heavy whipping cream (33–35%)

+ 350–400 g Savoiardi (ladyfinger biscuits)

#### SOAKING SYRUP

•200–250 g espresso
•150 g Baileys

~ 15–20 g cocoa powder for dusting

### INSTRUCTIONS

1. Mix the yolks and sugar into a heatproof bowl, and set over a hot water bath.

2. Stir constantly with a whisk, heat to 60°C (~140°F), and hold over a water bath for another 2–3 minutes to pasteurize the yolks.

3. Transfer to a mixer bowl, let cool to room temperature, and beat until the yolks turn light and pale and increase in volume.

4. Add mascarpone and vanilla seeds; mix until smooth.

5. In a separate bowl, whip the cold heavy whipping cream to soft peaks, then gently fold into the yolk mixture with a spatula.

6. For the soak, stir Baileys into the espresso.

7. Spread a thin layer of frosting on the bottom of your dish. Quickly dip half (~175–200 g) of the Savoiardi biscuits in the coffee mixture and arrange as the first layer.

8. Spoon and evenly spread half of the frosting on top of the biscuits. Soak the remaining biscuits and form the second layer.

9. Finish with the rest of the frosting, smooth the top, and dust generously with cocoa powder.

10. Chill in the fridge for at least 10–12 hours for the Tiramisu to set.

## CHOCO–PEANUT BUTTER CUPCAKES RECIPE

A flavor that resonates with many — chocolate, salted caramel, and peanut butter.

### Ingredients

#### BATTER (yields~12–14cupcakes)

120g boiling water
160g all-purpose flour
200g granulated sugar
50g Dutch processed cocoa powder 22–24%
1tsp instant espresso powder
½tsp baking powder
½tsp baking soda
½tsp salt
120g sour cream 20%
75g dorless oil
2large eggs (Grade A)
1tsp vanilla extract

#### CARAMEL (yields~220g)

110g granulated sugar
20g water
20g glucose syrup
130g heavy whipping cream 33–35%
35g butter 82%
2g salt

#### FROSTING (yields~650 g)

180g cream cheese 65%
220g mascarpone 80%
150g heavy whipping cream 33–36%
55g peanut butter
40g powdered sugar
1tsp fleur de sel or ⅓ tsp salt
1tsp vanilla extract

### Instructions

#### BATTER

1.Preheat the oven to 160°C (~320°F) and line a cupcake tray with paper liners (large, 60–70 g of batter each)
2.Boil and weigh 120 g of water; let it cool slightly.
3.In a large bowl, combine all dry ingredients using a hand whisk
4.Add all wet ingredients, including the boiled water, in a separate bowl, and whisk until smooth
5.Slowly pour the wet mixture into the dry, gently mixing until thoroughly combined with no lumps
6.Fill cupcake liners about ⅔ full and bake for 20–25 minutes. Let cool in the tray for 5 minutes, then remove and cool completely on a wire rack

#### CARAMEL

Place sugar, water, and glucose syrup in a saucepan and begin heating. Simultaneously bring the heavy whipping cream to a boil in a separate pot. Once the sugar syrup turns golden (~170–180°C), stirring constantly, slowly add the hot cream. Add soft butter, salt, and mix well. Cover with plastic wrap (touching the entire surface) and let cool

#### FROSTING

Combine all ingredients in a mixer bowl and whip until stable and the frosting texture leaves clear whisk marks

#### ASSEMBLY

Once the cupcakes have cooled, use the back of a large piping tip to carve out a hole in the center. Fill each cupcake with 1 tsp of caramel and 1 tsp of peanut butter. Plug the hole with a small piece of cake, then pipe a swirl of cream on top using a closed star tip.

## HAZELNUT PRALINE RECIPE

I'm sharing my trusted recipe for vanilla praline with you — and if you're saving it, be sure to thank me with a ❤️ and leave a comment!

It's quick to make, has a long shelf life, and tastes amazing in frostings, fillings, ganaches — or even just eaten by the spoonful!

### Ingredients

✔️250 g hazelnuts ✔️2 used vanilla pods ✔️120 g sugar ✔️45 g glucose syrup ✔️35 g water ✔️1 tsp Fleur de Sel or ½ tsp regular salt

### Instructions

Place hazelnuts and vanilla pods in the oven at 140°C (~285°F) for 15 minutes.
Meanwhile, combine sugar, glucose syrup, and water in a saucepan and heat the syrup to 118°C (~244°F).
Once the syrup reaches this temperature, add vanilla pods and hot nuts, and begin caramelizing.
At a certain point, your syrup will completely crystallize. Don't worry — that's normal! Keep cooking until the sugar melts again and turns into a dry caramel.
Pour the caramelized hazelnuts onto a tray (ideally lined with a silicone mat or parchment paper), sprinkle with fleur de sel, and let cool.
Break into pieces and transfer to a blender or food processor. Blend until it becomes a smooth paste.

Yield: ~380 g of praline.

By the way, you can substitute almonds or peanuts for hazelnuts.

## PERFECT ORANGE COMPOTE RECIPE

This filling turns out incredibly fragrant and delicious — the rich citrus flavor of orange is perfectly complemented by the exotic tang of passion fruit.

If you're saving the recipe, please leave a comment below — it would make me very delighted! 🥰

### Ingredients

100 g orange pulp (1)
55 g passion fruit purée
55 g sugar
3.5 g NH pectin
2 g orange zest
3 g gelatin (160 Bloom)
18 g cold water
50 g orange pulp (2)

### Instructions

1. Pour the passion fruit purée into a saucepan and add the orange pulp (1). Place over medium heat.
2. Mix the sugar thoroughly with the pectin, then gradually sprinkle it into the saucepan while stirring constantly. Add the orange zest.
3. Bloom the gelatin by soaking it in cold water.
4. Bring the fruit mixture to a boil, then remove from the heat. Add the gelatin and stir until it is fully dissolved.
5. Add the remaining orange pulp (2). Cover the entire surface of the compote directly with plastic food wrap and chill until it has stabilized.

Optional: You can add a used vanilla bean pod or 20–25 g of the candied orange peel from the previous recipe.

This filling is perfect for sponge cakes, mousse cakes, macarons, choux pastries, and more.

## RECIPE FOR TIRAMISU CUPCAKES ☕️🧁

### Ingredients

#### INGREDIENTS (for 12 cupcakes):

· 150 g all-purpose flour
· 50 g almond flour
· ¼ tsp baking soda
· ¼ tsp baking powder
· ¼ tsp salt
· 70 g softened butter
· 200 g sugar
· 3 large eggs
· 150 g sour cream (15–20%)
· 18 g odorless oil
· 1 tsp vanilla extract
· 1 tbsp instant coffee

#### FROSTING:

· 500 g mascarpone
· 250 g heavy whipping cream (33–36%)
· 40 g powdered sugar

## STABILIZING WHIPPED CREAM 🥣

**NOTE:** This entry is more of a technique/reference guide than a standard recipe (no single dish is being made) but it is clearly demarcated as its own titled unit in the source, distinct from neighboring recipes, so it is included as its own entry per the instruction to identify distinct content units by subject change.

☀️ In warm weather, adding gelatin or a stabilizer to heavy whipped cream can be beneficial for maintaining its shape.

❕ This is especially important for dishes featuring heavy whipped cream that will be displayed or served on a buffet table. Don't forget to hit ❤️ and save this post so you can easily refer to it when needed. 👌🏻

### Instructions

#### HOW TO USE A STABILIZER 👇🏻

Sift the stabilizer along with the powdered sugar you'll use to sweeten the heavy whipped cream.

🥣 Use approximately 7 g of stabilizer per liter of heavy whipped cream, or follow the package instructions. Add the sugar and stabilizer during the whipping process.

#### GELATIN 👇🏻

For gelatin, use the following proportions:

▫️ 1 liter of heavy whipped cream
▫️ 10 g of gelatin
▫️ 60 ml of cold water

🥣 Soak the gelatin in cold water, then heat it until fully dissolved. Whip the cream until it begins to thicken, then gradually and evenly pour in the gelatin while continuing to whip to the desired consistency.

## RECIPE FOR "VANILLA PANA-COTTA" CHEESECAKE

An incredibly tender dessert bursting with vanilla flavor while it melts in your mouth!

A crunchy base with crushed nuts and desiccated coconut combined with a delicate creamy cheesecake layer creates a unique taste that will be remembered for a long time.

### Ingredients

#### CRUST

215 g basic crumb (recipe you can find in my stories, also will be pinned later in the highlights)
35 g desiccated coconut
30 g hazelnuts
30 g almonds
32 g coconut oil
15-25 g butter

#### CREAM CHEESE LAYER

900 g cream cheese
250-300 g sugar (to taste)
50 g cornstarch
330 g whole eggs
650 g heavy cream (33-36%)
20 g vanilla paste
40 g lemon juice

#### PANA-COTTA

5.5 g gelatin + 33 g water
400 g heavy cream (30%)
20 g sugar
1 vanilla bean

### Instructions

#### CRUST

📌 Slightly toast the nuts until golden brown, then let it cool and finely chop.
📌 Grind in pulses the crumb into fine crumbs in a food processor.
📌 Mix the crumbs with the nuts and desiccated coconut. Add softened butter and coconut oil and combine until homogeneous.
📌 Line the bottom of a cake form (mine is 20*30 cm) tightly with foil. Form the cheesecake base by pressing the shortcrust crumbs firmly onto the bottom. Place the cheesecake crust in a freezer for at least 30 minutes.

#### CREAM CHEESE LAYER

📌 Beat cream cheese, cornstarch, and sugar in a mixer bowl with a paddle attachment until creamy.
📌 Gradually add eggs and mix well. Add heavy cream, vanilla paste, and lemon juice, and mix until smooth.
