## HAZELNUT-CHOCOLATE CUPCAKE RECIPE 🌰

#marusya_recipe

### Ingredients

#### 🔸 Crumble

100 g hazelnuts
80 g caster sugar
3 g cinnamon powder
1/4 tsp. salt

#### 🔸 Batter ~28-30 pcs.

250 g sour cream 20-30%
130 g Greek yogurt 10-12%
1.5 tsp. baking soda
180 g whole eggs (~3 pcs)
200 g caster sugar
250 g butter 82%
1 tbsp. rum
2 tsp. vanilla paste
460 g all-purpose flour
2 tsp. baking powder
1 tsp. salt

#### 🔸Ganache

240 g milk chocolate 33%
80 g hazelnut paste
200 g heavy cream 33-36% (1)
250 g heavy cream 33-36% (2)
300 g mascarpone 80-85%

### Instructions

#### ✔️ Crumble

📌 Roast the hazelnuts in the oven until golden brown and a pronounced nutty flavor, then cool down completely.

📍 if you're using unskinned hazelnuts, skin them after roasting

📌 Put the roasted hazelnuts, sugar, cinnamon powder and salt into the bowl of a blender / food processor. Grind in pulses into fine crumbs.

#### ✔️ Batter

📌 Combine sour cream and yogurt with baking soda and set the mixture aside for 10-15 min. Place the eggs and sugar in a blender bowl and beat for a minute. Add soft butter, and beat again until smooth.

📌 Add rum and vanilla paste to the sour cream and yogurt mixture, mix. Pour the mixture into a blender bowl and carefully process in pulses until smooth. Transfer the mixture in a large bowl.

📌 Sift the flour, mix it with baking powder and salt, add to liquid ingredients and mix thoroughly with a silicone spatula.

📌 Transfer the batter to a piping bag and pipe it into paper molds, filling them 1/3 full. Sprinkle ~1 tsp. of crumble on top of each cupcake. Pipe the second layer of batter on top, filling the paper molds 3/4 full. Sprinkle the cupcakes with the remaining hazelnut crumble, pressing it lightly with a teaspoon into the batter.

📌 Bake in the oven, preheated to 180-200 °C, for about 15 min. Check the doneness with a dry skewer. Remove from the oven, allow to cool down completely.

## APRICOT-AMARETTO CHEESECAKE RECIPE 🍑

#marusya_recipe

Summer, like the season of apricots, has come to an end, but the team and I can't miss the opportunity to share with you a recipe for the delicate Apricot-Amaretto cheesecake.

If apricots are not available, I advise you to replace them with sweet peaches or nectarines and treat yourself to an easy-to-prepare, but very tasty dessert.

Don't forget that your like ❤️ before bookmarking the recipe is the best gratitude for our humble work!

So, let's get back to the recipe. To prepare this cheesecake, you'll need a cake ring (d = 22-24 cm, h = 6 cm).

### Ingredients

#### 🔸 Crust

200 g shortcrust crumble or biscuits
80 g almond flakes
10 g desiccated coconut
40-50 g butter 82%

#### 🔸 Cream cheese layer

850 g cream cheese 60-70%
160 g caster sugar
280 g whole eggs (~5 pcs)
85 g sour cream 20-30%
70 g of Amaretto liquor
1 tsp. vanilla paste
zest of one orange

#### 🔸 Decoration

400 g fresh apricots
1 tbsp. caster sugar
1 tbsp. rum
1 tbsp. Amaretto liquor
15-20 g almond flakes

### Instructions

#### ✔️Crust

📌 Lightly roast almond flakes in the oven at 160-170 °C for about 5-6 min until golden brown, cool and crush them with your hands.
Grind the crumble in a food processor until fine crumbs have formed.
📌 Mix ground crumbs with crushed almond flakes and desiccated coconut. Add soft butter and mix until smooth.
📌 Wrap the bottom of the cake ring tightly with foil. Form the cheesecake crust by pressing the mixture firmly into the bottom. Place it in the freezer for at least 30 min.

#### ✔️ Cream cheese layer

📌 Mix the cream cheese with sugar at low speed until creamy, then gradually add the eggs and mix thoroughly until smooth. Add sour cream, pour in the Amaretto liquor and mix again. Then add vanilla paste, orange zest, and mix.
📌 Take the ring with the crust out of the freezer and pour in the cream cheese mixture. Bake the cheesecake for about 100-120 min at 120-130 °C with a water bath.

## ANANA TEA CAKE WITH DATES AND NUTS

Catch the recipe for an amazingly delicious, yet easy-to-make tea cake. If you serve it with buttercream and caramel-banana sauce — you'll definitely be paid high compliments!

The recipe for caramel sauce makes quite a large portion, but it's just perfect for waffles, crepes, pancakes, or just to accompany your coffee or tea.

Those who are wild about banana and caramel flavors, please, welcome!

#marusya_recipe

### Ingredients

#### 🔸Tea Cake

260 g chopped dates
200 g brown sugar
80 g butter 82%
60 g dark rum
180 g water
1/2 tsp. baking soda
80 g whole eggs
130 g bananas
1 tsp. vanilla paste
100 g walnuts
1 tsp. salt
260 g all-purpose flour
10 g baking powder

#### 🍌 Caramel-Banana Sauce

125 g caster sugar
50 g glucose syrup
150 g heavy cream 33-36%
70 g banana puree
1 tsp. vanilla paste
15 g dark rum
60 g butter 82%
1/3 tsp. salt

#### 🔸 Buttercream for decoration:

265 g cream cheese
135 g heavy cream 33-36%
30 g icing sugar
3 g vanilla paste

2 rectangular molds ~22x9 cm, h=10 cm

### Instructions

#### ✔️ Tea cakes

📌 Lightly roast the walnuts in the oven at 170-180 °C for about 5-6 minutes until light golden color and pleasant aroma. Let them cool down and coarse chop them.

📌 Put sugar, butter, water, rum and chopped dates in a saucepan. Stirring occasionally, heat the mixture until sugar and butter have dissolved. Then increase the heat and bring the mixture to a boil. 📌 Grease the molds with butter and dust them with some flour. Pour the batter into the molds, filling them approximately ⅔ (~ 680 g of batter per 1 tea cake).

### Ingredients

#### 🔸 Cake Layers

6 g baking soda
3 g citric acid

#### 🍓 Strawberry Confit

250 g strawberry puree
1 tbsp. lemon/lime juice

#### 🔸 Caramel Frosting

100 g butter 82%
370 g caramel condensed milk

#### 🍏 Apple jam

### Ingredients

#### Shortcrust base:

100 g of flour
70 g of whole grain flour
100 g of cold butter
2 tbsps of ice water
1 egg yolk
a pinch of salt

### Instructions

#### Shortcrust base

📌Mix the dry ingredients. Add the cold diced butter and turn everything into a crumble with a mixer (with a paddle attachment) or chop it with a knife.
📌Add the yolk, water and salt. As soon as the dough can be shaped into a ball, stop mixing it. Slightly roll out the dough, wrap it in plastic wrap and put in the fridge for 1-2 h.
📌 Roll out the dough into a layer 3-4 mm thick and put it in a mold d=18 cm, making the borders 4-5 cm high, prick with a fork and put it in the freezer for 10-15 min.
📌 Put a sheet of parchment paper on top of the dough in the mold and fill it with some weight (e.g. beans or lentils).
📌Bake it in a preheated oven for 15 min with the weight on and 10 min without it, until the base is lightly browned.
⠀

### Ingredients (continued)

#### Filling:

250 g of salmon fillet
200 g of broccoli
70 g of gorgonzola
⠀

### Instructions (continued)

#### Filling

📌 Dice the salmon, removing the pin bones. Crumble the cheese with your hands into small pieces.
📌 Disassemble the broccoli into separate inflorescences, cut large ones into pieces and cook them for 1-2 min in boiling salted water. Pour into a colander to drain.
⠀

### Ingredients (continued)

#### Egg and sour cream mixture:

3 eggs
250 g of sour cream 15%
salt, pepper to taste *
⠀

### Instructions (continued)

#### Egg and sour cream mixture

📌Mix all ingredients until homogeneous.
📌Put the filling into the base, pour the egg and sour cream mixture on top. Bake at 200°C/392°F for about 40-50 min. The filling should set.
📌 Let the quiche cool down for 20 min.

* Be sure to taste your cheese before adding salt to the egg and sour cream mixture. If it's too salty, then don't add salt to it.
